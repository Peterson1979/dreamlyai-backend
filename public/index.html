Core Application Features

Explore the comprehensive dream interpretation and journaling capabilities built into Dreamly AI.

Structured AI Interpretations

Receive balanced 6-part dream interpretations covering Summary, Detailed Analysis, Symbolic Meaning, Emotional Tone, Event Sequence, and Open-Ended Reflection.

Global Multilingual Support

Full localization and native-language interpretations across 32 world languages, including English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.

Personal Dream Journal

Log your dreams immediately upon waking. Categorize entries by dream type (Vivid, Lucid, Nightmare, Recurring, Flying, Falling) and assign vividness ratings.

Mood & Emotion Tagging

Tag the dominant feelings experienced in your dreams—joy, peace, anxiety, wonder, or intensity—to track your emotional journey over time.

Calendar & Recurring Patterns

Explore your dream history through a calendar view and discover recurring symbols, common themes, lucid dream rates, and overall dream frequency.

On-Device Voice Notes

Record quick audio voice notes to capture dreams before morning memories fade. Voice recordings remain stored securely on your local device.

On-Device Privacy & Data Protection

Dreamly AI is designed with user privacy, local storage, and data minimization as foundational principles:

  • Journal entries and voice notes are stored locally on your Android device
  • No cloud user account or mandatory sign-up required
  • Secure HTTPS / TLS encryption for all AI interpretation requests
  • Full control to edit, export, or erase your dream records at any time
